## [supply_chain] Supply Chain & Market Ethics

### Adulteration & Identity Risks
**Risk Level:** **Moderate**
*   **L-DOPA Standardization:** The primary quality metric. Natural extracts typically range from **15% to 50% L-DOPA**.
*   **Synthetic Spiking:** Some "99% L-DOPA" products are actually synthetic levodopa labeled as herbal mucuna. True full-spectrum extracts rarely exceed 50-60% L-DOPA without isolation.
*   **Species:** *Mucuna pruriens* var. *pruriens* (wild) vs. var. *utilis* (cultivated). Both are used, but L-DOPA content can vary.

### Sustainability & Environmental Impact
**Status:** **High Sustainability (Nitrogen Fixer)**
*   **Cultivation:** Mucuna is a vigorous legume often used as a **cover crop** and **green manure** to fix nitrogen and improve soil health. It is highly sustainable and beneficial for agroecosystems.
*   **Sourcing:** Primarily sourced from India and Africa.

### Certification & Sourcing Recommendations
*   **Standardization:** Look for **15-25% L-DOPA** for a balanced full-spectrum effect, or higher (40-50%) for targeted dopaminergic therapy.
*   **Testing:** HPLC verification of L-DOPA content is essential.

## Clinical Overview

### Quick Reference
**Primary Clinical Uses:**
- **Parkinson's Disease:** Natural alternative or adjunct to synthetic levodopa (reduces "off" periods).
- **Male Infertility:** Increases sperm count, motility, and testosterone.
- **Hyperprolactinemia:** Lowers prolactin via dopamine agonist activity.
- **Mood:** Supports motivation and focus (dopamine).

**Evidence Grade:** B (RCTs for Parkinson's and Infertility)

**Key Mechanism:**
- Contains **natural L-DOPA**, the direct precursor to dopamine.
- Crosses the blood-brain barrier to increase brain dopamine levels.

## Botanical Identity and Traditional Use

### Nomenclature
**Scientific Name:** *Mucuna pruriens* (L.) DC.
**Family:** Fabaceae (Legume Family)
**Common Names:** Velvet Bean, Cowhage, Kapikacchu (Ayurveda).

### Traditional Use
- **Ayurveda:** "Atmagupta" or "Kapikacchu". Used for *Kampavata* (Parkinson's-like tremors) and *Vajikarana* (Aphrodisiac/Virility).
- **TCM:** Used as a tonic.
- **Food:** Beans are eaten after processing (boiling) to remove anti-nutrients.

## Phytochemistry

### Primary Bioactive: L-DOPA
- **Levodopa (L-3,4-dihydroxyphenylalanine):** 3-7% in raw seeds; concentrated in extracts.
- **Bioavailability:** Some studies suggest natural mucuna L-DOPA has better bioavailability and tolerability than synthetic levodopa/carbidopa, possibly due to other phytochemicals (chelators, enzyme inhibitors) in the seed.

### Other Constituents
- **Alkaloids:** Mucunine, mucunadine.
- **Saponins & Sterols:** May contribute to hormonal effects.
- **Tryptamines:** Trace amounts (serotonin, 5-HTP) found in pods/hairs, usually negligible in seed extracts.

## Clinical Evidence (Grade B)

### Parkinson's Disease (Grade B)
**Findings:**
- **Double-blind Crossover Trial (Katzenschlager et al., 2004):** Mucuna seed powder (30g) showed **faster onset of action** and **longer "on" time** compared to standard synthetic levodopa/carbidopa in PD patients.
- **Tolerability:** Often fewer dyskinesias reported than with synthetic levodopa equivalents in long-term use.
- **Mechanism:** Direct dopamine replacement.

### Male Infertility & Hormones (Grade B)
**Findings:**
- **Sperm Quality:** Multiple studies show significant increases in sperm count, motility, and volume in infertile men.
- **Hormonal Profile:** Increases **Testosterone** and **LH**; Decreases **FSH** and **Prolactin** (dopamine inhibits prolactin).
- **Stress:** Reduces cortisol levels in stressed infertile men (Shukla et al., 2010).

## Safety Profile and Contraindications

### Adverse Effects
- **GI:** Nausea, vomiting, bloating (common with L-DOPA).
- **Neuro:** Dyskinesia (involuntary movements) at high doses, insomnia, anxiety, agitation.
- **CV:** Orthostatic hypotension (dopamine effect).

### Contraindications
- **MAO Inhibitors:** **ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATION**. Risk of hypertensive crisis.
- **Melanoma:** L-DOPA can theoretically activate malignant melanoma cells.
- **Psychosis/Schizophrenia:** Dopamine can exacerbate symptoms.
- **Glaucoma:** Narrow-angle glaucoma (dopamine can affect intraocular pressure).
- **Pregnancy:** Avoid.

---

## Drug Interactions

- **MAOIs (Phenelzine, Selegiline):** Dangerous hypertensive crisis. Stop MAOIs 2 weeks before mucuna.
- **Antipsychotics (Haloperidol, Risperidone):** Antagonize effects (dopamine blockers vs. dopamine booster).
- **Antihypertensives:** Potentiate hypotension.
- **Vitamin B6:** High doses of B6 can increase peripheral conversion of L-DOPA, reducing brain availability (unless taking carbidopa).

## Dosing Guidelines

| Indication | Formulation | Dose (L-DOPA equivalent) | Frequency | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Parkinson's** | Standardized Extract | Titrated (e.g., 100-600mg L-DOPA) | Divided doses | **Medical Supervision Required**. |
| **Infertility** | Seed Powder/Extract | 5g Powder or 500mg Extract | Daily | 3-6 months duration. |
| **Mood/Focus** | Standardized Extract | 60-100mg L-DOPA | Daily (AM) | Cycle use (e.g., 5 days on, 2 off). |
| **Prolactin** | Standardized Extract | 200-400mg Extract | Daily | Monitor prolactin levels. |

**Note:** Start low to assess tolerance (nausea). Take with food to reduce GI upset, but avoid high-protein meals (competes for absorption).
